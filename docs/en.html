Seg34-C11

A Chinese edition of Segment34 Mk3 for Garmin AMOLED and MIP watches. Large digital numerals show the hours and minutes, with the weekday, lunar date and solar-term countdown below. Adjust the layout and preview colors on the watch.

Top to bottom: 7-day trend graph, two weather lines, large time display with side progress bars, Chinese lunar and solar-term date bar, and daily metrics below. Click either dial for a larger view.

How the dial reads at a glance

Weather appears at the top, hours and minutes in large digital numerals at the center, and the date below.

Two dedicated weather lines

Choose Wings or a trend graph at the top. Models with weather support have two weather lines with four customizable metrics (such as temperature, wind, humidity, feels-like, or precipitation chance) and a clean English condition line like PARTLY CLOUDY, never crowded by the calendar.

Lunar date and solar-term countdown

The month, day, weekday and lunar date appear below the time. The solar-term countdown is dimmed until the day of the solar term, when it brightens. Show the weekday in Chinese or English, or hide it.

Side seconds & extra status slot

Two-digit seconds sit beside the clock, opening a separate status slot at the end of the date line—defaulting to Move Bar level, or switchable to stress, Body Battery, recovery time, or notifications—independent of the two vertical side bars.

Sharper lower dial & icons

AMOLED models use bold, high-contrast lower labels with selectable Dot, Hybrid, or Lines numerals, paired with larger battery and moon-phase icons, crisp pixel notification badges, and matched English/Chinese training status colors.

Seg34 Block-Round Chinese bitmap glyphs

Drawn pixel by pixel for the 45 characters used across weekdays, lunar dates, and 24 solar terms in two pure 1-bit sizes—24 px for most AMOLED watches and 15 px for 360×360 AMOLED models and MIP models—keeping dense strokes crisp alongside the time display.

Find settings by dial position, preview palettes live

Everyday customization lives right on the watch, organized top-to-bottom into 5 spatial groups (in Chinese on the watch) matching the dial layout, while keeping full Garmin Connect Mobile support.

Spatial on-watch settings menu

Open watch-face Customize to see all 5 zones in dial order. Enter any group—such as Top Weather (顶部天气)—to adjust the graph or fields in that area.

Live full-dial palette preview

Choosing Day or Night palette on the watch previews the entire dial live: scroll through the color ring on the left, then confirm or cancel on the right before applying.
